Welcome to Dibs

Before you start, in plain words:

  • Your resume, notes and tracker stay in your browser on this computer. Dibs has no server and no account.
  • Dibs reads a page when you open its panel there or press Read this page. It reads job pages on its own only if you allow that later.
  • It contacts GitHub only if you enter a username, and passes text to an AI only when you press a button for it.
  • You send your own applications and check that every resume line is true.
  • Scores are keyword matches, not guarantees.
